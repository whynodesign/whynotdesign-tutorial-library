# Voice and tone

TONEROAST sounds like the roaster talking you through a batch: calm, exact, a little dry.

## Principles

| Trait | Not | So the writing is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Patient | slow | **Measured.** Short sentences. One fact per sentence. |
| Rhythmic | musical-cute | **Counted.** Numbers do the work: rounds, hours, rest days, level. Never "symphony", "melody of flavour", "交響". |
| Smoky-warm | rustic | **Sensory and plain.** Name the smell or texture (焦糖、木質、炭香). No "ancient tradition". |
| Precise | technical | **Logged.** Every claim maps to the roast log. If it is not in the log, don't say it. |

## Languages

- **繁體中文 first, English second.** Traditional characters only. **Never Simplified** (烏龍 not 乌龙, 樂譜 not 乐谱, 台灣 not 台湾).
- Chinese and English are **parallel, not translations of each other's adjectives.** Each side may be shorter.
- On a surface: Chinese line above, English line below, English in the mono or wordmark voice.
- Between Chinese and Latin/numerals inside one line, put **one normal space** (`焙度 4`, `6 回`, `50 g`).
- The separator is **space · space** (U+0020 U+00B7 U+0020). Never `/`, `|`, `-`, or a full-width `・`.
- Numerals are always Arabic and set in DM Mono (`4`, not `四`, on product surfaces).
- Units: `h` for hours, `日` for days in Chinese, `d` in English-only contexts; `g` for weight, with a space.

## Tone by surface

| Surface | Tone | Length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Front panel | Data only. No sentences. | Whitelist strings only |
| Back panel | Log + one short note from the roaster | ≤ 60 字 + log table |
| Poster | One headline + data | Headline ≤ 6 字 |
| Social | One fact about this batch | ≤ 2 sentences per language |
| Motion | Wordmark only | — |

## Examples

Good — 「第 4 回後歇了 10 日。炭香收進去了，甜味浮上來。」 / "Rested 10 days after round 4. The smoke settled; the sweetness came up."

Bad — 「百年炭焙工藝，譜寫東方茶韻交響曲。」 (heritage claim, music metaphor, no data)

## Rules

- Never: 傳承百年, 古法, 秘方, 禪, 交響, artisan, premium, symphony, ancient, zen, secret.
- No health or medical claims. No "organic" without certification. No invented awards, origins or people.
- No exclamation marks. No emoji on product surfaces.

## When the rules don't cover it

Write the sentence as the roaster would say it to one customer standing at the counter, then cut every
adjective that is not a smell, a texture or a number.
