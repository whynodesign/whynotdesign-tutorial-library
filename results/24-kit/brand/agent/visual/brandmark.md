# Brandmark

All geometry: `visual-system.json` → `mark`. All files: `assets/logos/` (generated by `tools/make_marks.py`; never edit by hand).

## The symbol

Five equal lines (`mark.lines`, weight `mark.line_weight`, gap `mark.line_gap`), three equal amber bars
stepping up through the lower three spaces from left to right, the top space empty, and the ember dot above the
top line at the right end. Built on a 240-unit width. Reading: a roast climbing round by round, with room left.

## The level marks

`level-1.svg` … `level-5.svg`: the same five lines, **one** amber bar centred on line *n* counted from the
bottom, from `mark.level_bar.x_start` to the right edge, plus the ember. These are SKU marks, not alternative logos.

## The wordmark and lockups

- `wordmark.svg`: 焙曲 (Noto Serif TC Bold, outlined) over TONEROAST (Lexend Exa 500, `--tracking-wordmark`),
  TONEROAST fitted to the ink width of 焙曲.
- `lockup-horizontal.svg`: symbol + wordmark. Gap = 1 line gap. **焙曲's ink runs from the top of line 5 to the
  bottom of line 2; TONEROAST's baseline sits on the bottom of line 1.** This is the type-on-the-stave rule in its
  purest form; keep it if you ever redraw.
- `lockup-stacked.svg`: symbol over wordmark, wordmark centred, gap = 1 line gap.

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `symbol-char.svg` | Default on rice paper / celadon |
| `symbol-reversed.svg` | On kiln char (file includes the char ground) |
| `symbol-mono-char.svg` / `symbol-mono-paper.svg` | One-colour print, embossing, foil-free blind deboss, fax-grade uses, **and any surface that already has an ember** |
| `*-small.svg` | Below `mark.small_threshold_px` / `small_threshold_mm`: lines double weight |
| `lockup-*` | Covers, posters, end frames |
| `clear-space.svg` | Diagram |

## Colour

Lines: kiln char (or rice paper when reversed). Bars: oolong amber, always. Dot: ember red, always — except in mono
versions where everything is one colour. **Never** recolour the bars per product, never put the symbol on amber or ember.

## Clear space

One line gap on every side (`mark.clear_space`), measured from the outermost ink, including the ember dot.

## Minimum size

`mark.min_size`: 48 px / 16 mm wide. Below `small_threshold` use the small files. Reversed in print: never below the
reversed minimum (thin paper lines on a heavy black fill in). These are estimates; confirm on a press proof.

## Misuse

No outlines, no rotation, no perspective, no curved lines, no extra lines, no gradient bars, no drop shadow,
no 7-line version, no second dot, no music symbols added, no AI-redrawn version.

## When the rules don't cover it

Use the mono symbol at a larger size before you invent a variant.
