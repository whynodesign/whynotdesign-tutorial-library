Files copied unmodified from the Google Fonts collection on the build machine (2026-11-01); licence texts
downloaded from github.com/google/fonts (ofl/lexendexa, ofl/dmmono).

Rule for every whynotdesign kit: **Chinese fonts are referenced, not shipped.** Foundry faces such as
方正 or 漢儀 usually forbid redistribution with a kit even when the client owns a licence; check each one.
