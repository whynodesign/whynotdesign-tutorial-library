# Art direction (photography and AI imagery)

**No photography is approved yet.** The Exp09 AI base photo (tea balls that read as yarn, fresh green leaves) was
rejected and is not in this kit. Until real photography exists, surfaces are typographic: paper, stave, type.

## When imagery is used

- **Subject:** the roast, not the landscape — charcoal and ash, the roasting basket, rolled roasted leaf (dark,
  tight, not fresh green), the liquor's amber colour in a plain glass, the carton itself.
- **Light:** soft directional daylight, long low shadows, matte surfaces. Never studio-glossy.
- **Colour:** grade into the palette; amber appears only where it is the tea liquor or the ember glow.
- **Composition:** one subject, off-centre, at least 40% empty ground for the stave and type to be composited onto.
- **No people's faces** (rights), no hands holding cups, no props from the negative list in `../../how-to-prompt.md`.
- **AI images** are concept plates only: text-free, mark-free, labelled as AI-generated in the file name
  (`*_AI.jpg`) and in the delivery note. Licence and ownership of AI output are not settled; replace before commercial use.

## The editing formula
Generate (text-free) → grade to palette → composite stave + type in code/Figma → QA (`how-to-prompt.md`).

## When the rules don't cover it
Leave the image out. The brand works without it.
