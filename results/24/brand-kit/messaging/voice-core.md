# Brand Voice: Core Definition
TONEROAST sounds like a roaster talking you through the batch: calm, exact, a little dry.

## Personality → Tone
**Patient, not slow → Measured** — short sentences, one fact at a time.
**Rhythmic, not musical-cute → Counted** — use numbers (rounds, minutes, roast level), never "symphony", "melody of flavour".
**Smoky-warm, not rustic → Sensory and plain** — name the smell or texture; no "ancient tradition".
**Precise, not technical → Logged** — every claim maps to the roast log.

## Guardrails
- Never "ancient", "secret recipe", "zen", "symphony".
- Never health claims.
- zh-Hant first, English second; no simplified characters.
